## Rules

- **One slot.** If you want to change feature, edit your own line.
- **Be specific.** "Lava blob" or "health pack" — not "a monster". Two people
  building the same idea is a wasted workshop.
- **About half enemies, half items.** Items are quicker and easier; enemies are
  more visible in the game. Choose what you actually want to finish.
- **Handles only, no real names.** This repository is public; your GitHub handle
  is already public, your student details are not and should not be here.

## Ideas if you are stuck

**Enemies:** lava blob (leaves fire), bat (fast, erratic), knight (slow, tanky),
ghost (passes through trees), turret (stands still, shoots), slime king (splits in
two when killed), mushroom (poisons on touch), crab (moves sideways).

**Items:** health pack, speed potion, coin (score), bomb (clears nearby enemies),
magnet (pulls coins), shield (absorbs one hit), time slow, key, mystery box.
